Delete Your Account & Data

Last Updated: 24 August 2026

Overview

This page explains how users of the Overcome: Dopamine Reset for Men Android app can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

Account deletion is processed by email. You do not need to wait or qualify for anything — submit your request and we will confirm receipt within 48 hours, deactivate your account within 7 days, and remove your data from our live systems within 30 days.

Your privacy matters to us. All personal data associated with your account is deleted upon request, with the limited exceptions described below required by Indian law.
About backups. We keep encrypted backups on a rolling cycle so we can recover from a system failure. After your data is removed from our live systems within 30 days, a copy may still sit in a backup until that backup is overwritten, which takes up to 90 days in total. Backups are not accessible for ordinary use, are never used to restore a deleted account, and are only ever restored in the event of a system failure. We would rather tell you this than claim an instant erasure we cannot deliver.

How to Request Account Deletion

You can request deletion in two ways:

Option 1 — From inside the app (recommended)

  1. Open the Overcome app and go to your Profile Page.
  2. Tap "Request Account Deletion". This opens a pre-filled email addressed to overcomeapp0@gmail.com.
  3. Send the email without modifying the subject line.
  4. We will confirm receipt within 48 hours, deactivate the account within 7 days, and complete live deletion within 30 days.

Option 2 — By email directly

Send your request to
overcomeapp0@gmail.com
Subject line: "Data Deletion Request" — include the email address associated with your account

Upon receiving your request we will send a confirmation email. Your account and all associated data will be permanently removed from our live systems within 30 days of that confirmation, and from backups within 90 days.

What Is Retained After Deletion

A limited amount of data must be retained after account deletion as required by Indian law. This data cannot be deleted on request.
Data TypeRetention PeriodReason
Payment and transaction records7 yearsRequired under Indian accounting and tax law (Income Tax Act, GST regulations)

No recovery data, streak logs, relapse logs, chat messages, or habit-related personal data is retained in our live systems after deletion. Only the financial and correspondence records listed above are kept, and solely for legal compliance purposes.

Payment records are held by necessity in a form that links a transaction to an account. Where it is possible to do so without breaking the legal requirement, we reduce these records to the minimum the law requires.
