5. The Overcome Cashback Guarantee

The Cashback Guarantee is available on both plans. It is a performance-based cashback — not a no-questions-asked refund. The guarantee structure differs between plans. Read this section carefully before purchasing.

5.1 How the Guarantee Works

If you complete all required challenge weeks across your guarantee period and do not experience any meaningful improvement, you may claim 90% of the amount you paid back. The remaining 10% covers platform fees and programme infrastructure costs.

You decide whether you improved. Whether you experienced meaningful improvement is judged entirely by your own standards. We do not define improvement, do not measure it, and do not assess it. We will not ask you to justify your answer, will not request evidence, and will not reject a claim on the basis that we disagree with your view of your own progress. Your Clarity Score, streak length, and check-in ratings are not used to evaluate improvement and have no bearing on this part of the claim.

In practice this means there is exactly one objective test: did you complete every required week? If you did, and you tell us the programme did not meaningfully help you, the claim is approved.

5.2 What Counts as a Completed Week

A "completed week" is defined as a week in which every assigned challenge has met its individual completion threshold as recorded automatically by the app. Different challenges carry different thresholds — some require 3 out of 7 days, some 4 out of 7, and some 5 out of 7. These thresholds are displayed in the app for each challenge.

Tier 3 surprise challenges are excluded from the weekly completion calculation. Only Tier 1 and Tier 2 challenges count toward guarantee eligibility. Tier 3 challenges disappear on the day they appear and are not tracked for completion purposes.

Completion is determined solely by in-app logs. No external proof is required or accepted.

Completion logs cannot be edited by users. Once a day is recorded, you cannot change it, and neither the founding team nor anyone else can alter it at your request or at our own initiative to your disadvantage. This protects the integrity of the guarantee for everyone.

Correction of technical faults. The single exception is a verified technical fault on our side — for example a software bug, a failed synchronisation, a server outage, or a data migration error that caused the app to record something other than what you actually did. In that case we will correct the record so that it reflects your actual activity. Corrections are only ever made to reflect what genuinely happened; we will not add completions you did not earn, and a correction cannot reduce a completion you did earn. To report a suspected fault, email overcomeapp0@gmail.com with the subject line "Log Correction Request" as soon as you notice it, and in any event before your claim window closes.

Clarity Score. Your Clarity Score is calculated automatically by the app from your streak, challenge completions, lifestyle inputs, and consistency. It is a progress indicator only. It does not determine Cashback Guarantee eligibility, which depends solely on completed weeks as described in this Section. See Privacy Policy Section 2.7 for how this automated calculation works and how to request human review.
